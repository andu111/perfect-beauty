Privacy policy

Last updated 3 November 2026

This explains what personal information Perfect Beauty collects when you use this website or book an appointment, why, and what your rights are. In short: we only collect what we need to book and look after you, we never sell it, and you can ask us to see or delete it.

Who we are. Perfect Beauty is run by Alexandra as a sole trader, at 17 Marlborough Place, Banbury, Oxfordshire OX16 5DA. She is responsible for your information (the "data controller"). Contact: bookings@perfect-beauty.uk or +44 7717 114683.

What we collect

  • Booking details: your name, mobile number, email address, the treatment, date and time you choose, and anything you write in the notes box.
  • Deposit payments: if a deposit is needed, you pay on a secure page run by Stripe. We never see or store your card details. Stripe tells us that the payment worked and the amount.
  • Treatment notes: things you tell us at your appointment that we need to treat you safely, such as patch test results or allergies. If this includes health information, we only use it to look after you and keep it private.
  • Technical information: our web host keeps standard server logs (such as your device's internet address and the pages requested) for security. We use your internet address to stop spam and repeated booking attempts.

The virtual try-on

When you use the virtual try-on, your photo or camera picture is processed only on your own phone or computer. It is never uploaded to us or saved by us. To recognise the face, the page downloads Google's free face-tracking tool, which runs in your browser.

Why we use your information

  • To book and provide your treatment, including emails about your request, confirmation, changes and cancellations. This is needed to carry out the booking you ask for.
  • To take and refund deposits under our booking terms.
  • To keep business and tax records, which the law requires.
  • To keep the website safe from spam and misuse, which is in our legitimate interests.

We don't send marketing emails, and we don't use your information for advertising.

Photos of our work

We only show photos of clients in our portfolio with their permission. If you appear in a photo on this website and would like it removed, contact us and we'll take it down.

Who we share it with

Only with the services that help us run the business, and only what they need:

  • IONOS, which hosts this website and our email.
  • Stripe, which handles deposit payments.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr, which deliver the website's typefaces and the try-on face-tracking tool. Your browser contacts them directly, which shares your internet address with them.

We never sell your information. We would only share it with anyone else if the law requires it.

Cookies

The public website doesn't use tracking or advertising cookies. Stripe's payment page uses its own cookies to process payments safely and prevent fraud. Our staff booking area uses one cookie to keep staff signed in.

How long we keep it

  • Booking and payment records: for up to 6 years, because tax rules require business records to be kept.
  • Records of emails we've sent: 90 days.
  • Booking requests that were declined or never paid: we keep the basic details as part of our records, and you can ask us to delete them.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the information we hold about you;
  • correct anything that's wrong;
  • delete your information, unless we have to keep it for legal or tax reasons;
  • stop or limit how we use it, or object to it being used.

Email bookings@perfect-beauty.uk and we'll reply within one month. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk, though we'd appreciate the chance to sort it out first.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.
